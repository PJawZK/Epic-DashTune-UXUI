# EpicDash UX r19 concept-art radial restyling

Scope remains limited to the six primary radial gauges: RPM, Boost, AFR Gas Scale, Coolant, Oil Pressure and Fuel Level.

- Retains the r18 shared upper-half radial geometry and individual numeric ranges.
- Restyles cards, arcs, ticks, readouts, status strips and indicator markers toward the approved concept-art dashboard.
- Adds the concept-art symbol family as prominent centered icons, including an engine-speed icon for the RPM hero gauge.
- Adds a bounded pointer/marker to each radial gauge without changing value mapping.
- Preserves tablet-landscape structure and the separate responsive tablet-portrait, phone-portrait and phone-landscape profiles.
- Other dashboard cards and all non-dashboard pages remain unchanged.
- ECU access remains strictly read-only.
