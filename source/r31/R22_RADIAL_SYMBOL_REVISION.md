# EpicDash UX r22 radial-symbol revision

Scope is intentionally limited to the six primary radial-gauge symbol positions.

- RPM: removed the engine symbol entirely and used the recovered header area to enlarge/recenter the existing tachometer.
- Boost: newly drawn turbocharger housing/impeller SVG.
- AFR Gas Scale: newly drawn lambda SVG.
- Coolant: newly drawn thermometer and coolant-wave SVG.
- Oil Pressure: newly drawn oil-can and droplet SVG.
- Fuel Level: newly drawn fuel-pump SVG.

The r21 performance-optimized radial renderer, arc paths, zone bands, ticks, labels, pointer behavior and numeric mappings are locked and unchanged.
